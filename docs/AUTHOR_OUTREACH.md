# Author Outreach Guide

The goal is to **learn, not sell**. Ask how indie authors without a
maintained wiki feel about a spoiler-safe AI companion for their series, and
under what conditions they would welcome one, tolerate one, or object to one.

## Who to contact

- Long series (5+ books) with complex casts and **no official or maintained
  wiki**.
- A mix of distribution models:
  - Wide or direct sellers who already sell DRM-free EPUBs.
  - Kindle Unlimited exclusives (to learn whether the KU problem is a deal
    breaker).
  - Web-serial authors, for example on Royal Road.
- Start with about 5–10 conversations. Patterns usually show by around five.

## Context to share up front (be transparent)

- It's a personal project that started because you forget details between
  books and wikis spoil things or don't exist.
- How it works: it reads the books **you bought**, processes them on your own
  machine (or a service that doesn't keep or train on the data, if that is
  the setup), and answers questions using only what you've read so far, with
  quotes from the text.
- Nothing is published or shared. Nobody gets your text.
- You'd like to know how they feel before going any further.
- Mention that Amazon's "Ask this Book" now does something similar without an
  author opt-out. Their reaction to that is useful data in itself.

## Questions

**Gut reaction**
1. What's your first reaction to a tool like this?
2. Does anything about it worry you? What would make it feel wrong?

**Their readers**
3. Do readers ask you to recap, or ask "who was X again?" How often?
4. Have you thought about a wiki or series bible? What stopped you?
5. Do readers complain about forgetting details between releases? Does it
   affect read-through?

**Boundaries and consent**
6. Personal use (a reader running it on books they bought): fine, uneasy, or
   objectionable?
7. Shared use (one companion that all your readers can use): under what
   conditions?
8. Does it matter to you whether the AI runs locally or in the cloud?
9. Are there things you would want hidden or controlled (planned reveals,
   theories, unreleased worldbuilding)?

**Value and partnership**
10. Would an *official*, author-approved spoiler-safe companion for your
    series be useful to you? What would it need to have?
11. Would you want to review or correct its content? How much time would you
    realistically spend?
12. Would you point readers to it (newsletter, back matter)?
13. How do you feel about the different ways it could be paid for: free,
    reader pays, author pays, revenue share?

**Practical**
14. Do you sell DRM-free anywhere? Would you, for this?
15. Can I follow up later if I build something?

## Notes template (one per author)

```
Author / series:
Distribution: (KU / wide / direct / serial)
Has wiki?:
Gut reaction (1–5, hostile → enthusiastic):
Main concerns:
Conditions for comfort:
Interest in official companion (Y/N/maybe) + what they'd need:
Willing to share DRM-free files?:
Quotes worth keeping:
Follow-up OK?:
```

## What would count as a signal

- **Go (author-sanctioned path):** at least 3 out of ~8 authors are actively
  interested in an official companion and would review it or link to it.
- **Personal only:** authors are tolerant of personal use but don't care
  about an official version.
- **Rethink:** most authors object even to local personal use. This is
  unlikely, but if it happens, take it seriously.
